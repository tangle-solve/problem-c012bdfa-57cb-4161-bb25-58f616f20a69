### CHEM-000332: Predicting steel passivity after alkali-activated binder carbonation
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Predict steel depassivation from evolving pore solutions and hydrates during carbonation, chloride exposure and leaching.
- **Status:** A 2025 calcined-clay/slag study observed no corrosion after the carbonation front reached steel, consistent with retained high pH. Conventional front criteria therefore lack universal transferability.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Provides chemically valid durability criteria for alternative binders.
- **Key works:** S. D. C. Gomes et al. (2025), *Shrinkage and carbonation of alkali-activated calcined clay-ground granulated blast furnace slag (GGBFS) concrete*, [DOI:10.1016/j.cemconres.2025.107899](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cemconres.2025.107899).
